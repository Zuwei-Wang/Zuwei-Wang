## ⛳ Swing Plus

A golf scorekeeping app for iPhone. It's live on the App Store as **swing+**, and I keep shipping updates to it.

[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6832067116) · [Support](https://swingplus.stevenwangzv.workers.dev/support)

What it does:

- One big card per hole: tap left for −1, right for +1, swipe to change holes
- Keep score for the whole group on one phone (up to 4 players), with no accounts needed
- Match formats on top of stroke play: match play, Nassau, Skins, Stableford, team games and more, with handicap strokes
- Stats, a reference handicap, practice logging, and shareable score cards
- Lock Screen Live Activity, home and lock screen widgets
- Courses can be shared as files
- Syncs through the user's own private iCloud. No sign-up, no ads, no tracking
- Available in 8 languages

Built with SwiftUI, SwiftData, CloudKit, WidgetKit and ActivityKit.
